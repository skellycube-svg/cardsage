When the Green makes sense

Keep it if you travel frequently and eat out regularly. The 3x on both categories is simple and valuable. No category limits, no quarterly bonuses to track — just 3x on travel and dining, period.

The CLEAR Plus credit ($100 annual value) is incredibly useful if you fly 4+ times per year. CLEAR membership gives you expedited airport security lines — saves 10–15 minutes per airport. Over multiple flights, that's significant.

LoungeBuddy access (1,300+ lounges) gives you discounted or free lounge access without a full Priority Pass subscription. It's a nice bonus if you value lounge comfort while traveling.

When you should upgrade or downgrade

If you spend heavily on dining ($8,000+/year), upgrade to the Gold Card ($325/yr). You get 4x dining instead of 3x, plus $120 Uber cash and dining credits. The extra category benefit pays off at high spend levels.

If you spend less than $1,500/year on travel and dining combined, consider a no-fee card instead. The 3x earning won't justify the $150 fee at low spend.

Benefits worth noting

  • Transfer partners — 9 airline partners. Points worth 1.5–2¢ each vs. 0.6¢ as cash back.
  • Trip delay insurance — up to $300 per trip after 12-hour delay for meals and lodging.
  • Trip cancellation insurance — coverage for non-refundable travel.
  • Baggage insurance — up to $1,250 for carry-on, $500 for checked bags.

The transfer partners are the hidden gem. At 1.5¢ point value through partners, you're getting premium returns on your everyday spending.

The bottom line

The Amex Green is the best entry-level card for travelers who want simplicity and clean economics. 3x on travel and dining, CLEAR credit, lounge access — it all adds up to great value for frequent travelers. If you don't travel much, skip it.
