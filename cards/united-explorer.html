The short answer

The United Explorer is worth it if you fly United 2+ times per year or use the Instacart credit. Free checked bag for you and up to 8 companions saves $35 per trip ($70/year minimum). The $180 Instacart credit is incredibly useful for most people — that covers groceries every week. Add the $150 hotel credit and you're easily over $150 in pure credits. The card nearly breaks even on credits alone before you even earn miles.

When the United Explorer makes sense

Keep it if you use the Instacart credit alone. That $180 benefit covers about 4 months of modest grocery delivery spend. Most people use it, which means the card pays for itself before any other benefits kick in.

The free checked bag is a bonus for United flyers. If you take 2+ United flights per year, that's another $70+ saved.

The JSX flight credit is niche but valuable if you live in a JSX market (CA, TX, NV). JSX is a semi-private airline; the $150 credit covers a full flight.

When you should downgrade or cancel

If you never use Instacart and don't fly United, cancel it. Most of the value comes from the Instacart credit, and if you don't have that, the fee is hard to justify.

If you want a multi-airline United card without so many niche credits, consider the Venture X instead — better for general travel, worse for specific United perks.

Benefits worth noting

  • Instacart+ membership — the $180 credit includes a complimentary 3-month Instacart+ membership (free delivery on $35+ orders).
  • Trip cancellation insurance — up to $1,500 per person for non-refundable travel.
  • Baggage delay insurance — up to $100/day for 3 days if your baggage is delayed 6+ hours.
  • 3x on United flights — unlimited, uncapped miles earning on United bookings.

The Instacart+ membership alone makes the card useful if you order groceries even occasionally. Combined with the cash credit, it's hard to beat.

The bottom line

The United Explorer is a strong value card for the $150 fee. The Instacart credit alone justifies it for most people. If you fly United, the free bag is a bonus. If you live near JSX flights, even better. It's one of the few cards where the credits are so broad that almost everyone can extract value.
