When Southwest Priority makes sense

Keep it if you fly Southwest 4+ times per year. The free checked bag alone pays off at that frequency. Everything else is pure profit.

The free seat selection is especially valuable if you travel with family or groups. Being able to assign seats without paying extra fees adds up quickly.

The upgraded boarding passes are useful if you have specific seat preferences (exit row, aisle, front). 4 passes per year means you can board early on your most important trips.

When you should downgrade or reconsider

If you fly Southwest fewer than 4 times per year, cancel it. The free bag savings won't justify the $229 fee.

If you fly multiple airlines, this card only helps for Southwest. Consider a multi-airline card like the Venture X or CSR instead.

Southwest-specific perks

  • Companion pass potential — if you earn 110,000 RR points in a year, you get a free companion on any Southwest flight for a year.
  • Free seat selection — most airlines charge $15–25 per seat; Southwest's free selection saves you here.
  • Checked bag for companions — your checked bag is free + any companions in your reservation get theirs free too. No other airline matches this.
  • Trip protections — standard coverage for delays and cancellations.

The companion pass potential is a long-term benefit. If you spend enough on the card to earn 110,000 points, you unlock a valuable free companion pass.

The bottom line

The Southwest Priority is a no-brainer if you're committed to Southwest. The free bag benefit alone justifies the fee for frequent flyers. If you fly multiple airlines or Southwest rarely, skip it.
