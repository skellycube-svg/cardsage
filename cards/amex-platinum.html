When the Platinum makes sense

The Platinum is built for frequent travelers who stay at premium properties. If you book 3+ hotel nights per year at Fine Hotels + Resorts properties, the $300 hotel credit alone justifies half the fee. Add the airline credit, Uber cash, and Centurion lounge access, and the math works.

The card also makes sense if you're someone who already stays at these properties and would be paying the fee regardless. The 5x on flights and hotels booked through Amex Travel gives you 25% more points — which is real value if you transfer to airline partners like Delta or Singapore Airlines.

One often-overlooked benefit: Amex's concierge team will book restaurants, get upgrades, and arrange experiences. If you value that white-glove service, it has some intangible worth beyond the numbers.

When you should downgrade

If you're not staying at FHR properties regularly, downgrade to the Gold Card ($325/yr). You keep 4x dining, 4x groceries, the Membership Rewards points, and transfer partners. You lose the lounge access and hotel credit, but you also lose $570 in annual fees.

If you don't care about dining and grocery points at all, the Green Card ($150/yr) gives you 3x travel and 3x dining with $100 CLEAR Plus credit and LoungeBuddy access. Much simpler and way cheaper.

Benefits most people don't maximize

  • Airline selection — you can change which airline gets the $200 credit each year. Plan ahead.
  • Return protection — up to $300/item if a retailer won't accept a return within 90 days.
  • Trip cancellation insurance — up to $10,000 per trip for non-refundable expenses.
  • Cell phone protection — coverage up to $600 if your phone is damaged or stolen.
  • Baggage insurance — up to $3,000 for lost or damaged luggage per traveler.

These protections are valuable if you travel frequently or spend heavily through the card. But they only matter if something actually happens.

The bottom line

The Amex Platinum is the expensive choice. It works for the small percentage of people who travel a lot, stay at luxury hotels, and use the dining credits. For everyone else, the Gold Card or Green Card provide 80% of the value at 35–40% of the cost. The Platinum is about lifestyle and status, not pure ROI.
