When the Gold is worth keeping

Keep the Gold if you eat out 2+ times per week or spend $400+/month on groceries. Both of these behaviors are increasingly normal — eating out for lunch, DoorDash for dinner, grocery delivery. The 4x multiplier on all of it means you're earning at least $300+ per year in extra points beyond the fee.

The grocery cap is worth noting: you only earn 4x on the first $25,000/year in grocery spend. After that, it's 1x. For most people that's fine — $25k/year is about $2k/month. But if you have a large household and buy in bulk, you'll hit the cap.

When you should downgrade

If your dining and grocery spend is under $500/month combined, the Green Card ($150/yr) gives you 3x dining and travel plus $200 in credits. Saves you $175/year. Or if you don't want to think about categories at all, the Green Card is still better value.

If you never eat out or buy groceries on this card, cancel it entirely. The 4x benefit is what makes the Gold work — without it, you're overpaying.

Benefits that matter

  • Transfer partners — points worth 1.5–2¢ each to airlines and hotels, vs. 0.6¢ as cash back.
  • Return protection — retailer won't take it back? Amex covers up to $300/item.
  • Extended warranty — adds 1 extra year to manufacturer warranties (up to 5 years).
  • Trip delay insurance — up to $300 per trip after 12-hour delay.
  • Baggage insurance — up to $1,250 for carry-on, $500 for checked bags.

The transfer partners are the most valuable. If you transfer to partners like Singapore Airlines or Air France, your points are worth 50% more than cash back.

The bottom line

The Gold Card is the best mid-tier card for heavy dining and grocery spenders. If you eat out regularly and buy groceries, the 4x multiplier pays the fee and then some. If you don't, the Green Card is cheaper and simpler.
