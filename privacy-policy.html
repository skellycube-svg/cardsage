Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

CardSage is a personal finance tool that runs entirely in your browser. We take your privacy seriously and have designed the app to collect as little data as possible.

Data We Collect

CardSage stores the following data locally in your browser using localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to any server:

  • The cards you've added to your wallet
  • Benefits you've marked as used or redeemed
  • Your Card Finder quiz answers and results
  • Your email address, if you voluntarily subscribe to benefit reminders
No server. No account. No tracking. CardSage has no backend, no database, and no analytics. Your data lives only in your browser's localStorage and is deleted when you clear your browser data.

Email Address (Optional)

If you choose to subscribe to benefit reminders, you enter your email address voluntarily. This email is:

  • Stored in your browser's localStorage only
  • Sent to cardsage.co@gmail.com via a standard mailto: link, which opens your email client — no form submission or server is involved
  • Used only to send periodic reminders about monthly card credits
  • Never sold, shared, or used for any other purpose

Affiliate Links

CardSage contains affiliate links to credit card application pages. When you click "Apply Now" and are approved for a card, CardSage may earn a commission from the card issuer at no cost to you. Our recommendations are not influenced by affiliate relationships. See our full Affiliate Disclosure for details.

Third-Party Services

CardSage loads the following third-party resources to function:

  • Google Fonts — typography (Cormorant Garamond, Barlow, Source Code Pro)
  • unpkg.com — React and Babel via CDN

These CDN services may log your IP address as part of normal operations. We have no control over their data practices.

No Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with any third parties. The email address you optionally provide is used solely for benefit reminders sent directly from your own email client.

Deleting Your Data

All CardSage data lives in your browser's localStorage. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data, using developer tools to clear localStorage, or uninstalling the app if installed as a PWA.

Children's Privacy

CardSage is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Email cardsage.co@gmail.com.
