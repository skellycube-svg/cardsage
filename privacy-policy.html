Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

CardSage is a personal finance tool that runs entirely in your browser. We take your privacy seriously and have designed the app to collect as little data as possible.

Data We Collect

CardSage stores the following data locally in your browser using localStorage. If you do not sign in, this data never leaves your device:

  • The cards you've added to your wallet
  • Benefits you've marked as used, redeemed, or skipped
  • Your Card Finder quiz answers and results
  • Saved trips from the Plan tab
  • Your email address, if you voluntarily subscribe to benefit reminders
Local-first by default. If you never sign in, CardSage has no backend interaction — your data lives only in your browser's localStorage and is deleted when you clear your browser data.

Optional Cloud Sync (Firebase)

CardSage offers optional cloud sync so your data carries across devices and browsers. Cloud sync is entirely opt-in — it activates only when you sign in.

Authentication. Sign-in is powered by Firebase Authentication (a Google Cloud service). You can sign in with:

  • Google sign-in — we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from your Google account
  • Email and password — your password is hashed by Firebase and never visible to us

Data stored in the cloud. When signed in, the following data is synced to Cloud Firestore (a Google Cloud database):

  • Your card wallet selections (card IDs)
  • Benefit tracking state (checked and skipped benefits)
  • Benefit check dates

Security. All data transmitted between your browser and Firebase is encrypted in transit via TLS. Data stored in Firestore is encrypted at rest by Google Cloud. We do not store payment information, credit card numbers, or financial account credentials.

What we do NOT store in the cloud: quiz answers, saved trips, email subscription preferences, or any content you haven't explicitly synced by signing in.

Email Address (Optional)

If you choose to subscribe to benefit reminders, you enter your email address voluntarily. This email is:

  • Stored in your browser's localStorage only
  • Sent to cardsage.co@gmail.com via a standard mailto: link, which opens your email client — no form submission or server is involved
  • Used only to send periodic reminders about monthly card credits
  • Never sold, shared, or used for any other purpose

Affiliate Links

CardSage contains affiliate links to credit card application pages. When you click "Apply Now" and are approved for a card, CardSage may earn a commission from the card issuer at no cost to you. Our recommendations are not influenced by affiliate relationships. See our full Affiliate Disclosure for details.

Third-Party Services

CardSage loads the following third-party resources to function:

  • Google Firebase — authentication and cloud data sync (only when you sign in)
  • Google Fonts — typography (Playfair Display, Inter, Source Code Pro)
  • unpkg.com — React and Babel via CDN

These services may log your IP address as part of normal operations. Firebase is governed by Google's Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy. We have no control over their data practices beyond what they publicly document.

No Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with any third parties. Your cloud-synced data in Firebase is used solely to provide the sync feature and is not accessed for marketing, analytics, or any other purpose.

Deleting Your Data

Local data. All locally stored CardSage data lives in your browser's localStorage. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data, using developer tools to clear localStorage, or uninstalling the app if installed as a PWA.

Cloud data. If you signed in and want your cloud-stored data deleted from Firebase, email cardsage.co@gmail.com with the email address you used to sign in. We will delete your Firestore document and Firebase Authentication account within 30 days of your request.

Children's Privacy

CardSage is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy? Email cardsage.co@gmail.com.
